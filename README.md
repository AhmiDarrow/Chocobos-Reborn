# Chocobos Reborn

Final Fantasy VII chocobos for **Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.249**. Catch a wild
yellow with Gysahl Greens, train it on the eight greens, mate it with the eight nuts,
walk the farm line to Green, Blue, Black and Gold, and race at Chocobo Square.

Original work. Original birds, original wark, original art. Never commercial.

## The birds

Adults stand 3.25 m to the crest (about 1.8× a player). Chicobos hatch at a quarter of player height
and grow through three stages. Eight plumages: Yellow, Green, Blue, White, Black,
Gold, the End Chocobo and the Nether Chocobo. Females wear a shorter crest.

Craft a **Chocobo Almanac** (a book and a gysahl): the full guide, plus a page for every
bird you own with its stats, race record, genes and family line.

Wild yellows roam the Overworld; the Wonderful ones only show up on cold pads —
snow and ice. Nether Chocobos (red, lava in the feathers) are Nether-born; End
Chocobos (purple, end stone in the feathers) live on the End's outer islands. A **Chocobo Lure** in hand draws
wild birds toward you and outlines them so you can spot tracks across a field.

## Greens (training)

Gysahl, Krakka, Tantal, Pahsana, Curiel, Mimett, Reagan, Sylkis. Gysahl tames a
wild bird (about one in three tries) and heals a hurt one. Every green trains
speed, stamina, intelligence or cooperation; a bird takes one training green every
five minutes, and eventually gets sated on each kind, so move up the ladder. Gysahl
grows from seeds found in grass; the better greens come from Sage Wynn at the Square,
race prizes, and lucky harvests.

## Nuts (mating)

Pepio, Luchile, Saraha, Lasan, Pram, Porov, Carob, Zeio. Feed a nut to each of two
adults of opposite sex and they mate. Plain nuts hatch a chick of the parents'
colour whose talent follows the nut. **Carob** and **Zeio** change the line:

1. Two **Good**-or-better Yellows + Carob → **Green** (mountain) or **Blue** (river).
2. Green + Blue + Carob → **Black**. A missed roll hatches **White**.
3. Black + **Wonderful** Yellow + **Zeio** → **Gold**. Gold never hatches without Zeio.

As in FF7, colour breeding needs racers: both parents must have first-place
finishes at Chocobo Square before a Carob or Zeio can change the line (1 each for
Green/Blue, 2 each for Black, 3 each for Gold), and more wins make the roll surer
until it is certain (4 combined for Green/Blue, 9 for Black, 12 for Gold). A bird
that has not raced enough tells you so when you hand it the nut. Carob Nuts fall
from ravagers; Zeio Nuts from piglin brutes.

Every green also heals a hurt bird a little, restores stamina, grows a chick
faster, and a sated bird gets one feed of each green back per day.

## Riding

Craft a **Chocobo Saddle**, put it on an owned adult, and right-click to ride.
Sprint to dash on stamina; ease off to recover. Terrain follows FF7: Green birds
cross mountains (they climb), Blue birds cross rivers (they walk on shallow water
and give the rider water breathing), Black birds do both and see at night. Gold
birds do everything: mountains, any water, fire, night sight, slow self-healing, and they
fly (hold jump to flap, look up while moving to climb, hold sprint to dive; faster aloft than on
foot). End birds climb, cross any water and let the rider drift down; only Gold flies. Nether
birds shrug off fire and walk on lava. Sprint dives a flier; sneak ducks a water bird under.
You can only sneak-dismount on solid ground. Your crosshair passes through the bird while
riding, so doors and blocks work from the saddle; sneak to target the bird (empty hand opens
equipment).

## Chocobo Farm

Chocobo Farms dot the plains, meadows, savannas and cherry groves: a barn, a paddock with a couple
of wild yellows, a gysahl patch, a Stablehand in the barn selling greens and nuts, and
a Farmhand at the gate who can send you on to the Square, on foot or in the saddle.

## Whiskerwind

Whiskerwind is a race town on a sky island in the void, the Chocobo Square: a plaza round the
arrival medallion, a chocobo fountain, awninged market stalls, timber cottages, an inn with a
copper-capped bell tower, the Race Hall, a stable yard, a windmill, a gatehouse and pier over
the void, a shrine islet, its own birds wandering the streets, all under its own theme music.
Nothing spawns there on its own and nothing there can be dug up. Reach it with a **Chocobo Pocketwatch** or through the **Farmhand** at any Chocobo
Farm, on foot or in the saddle; your birds nearby come along.

* Heats go off every five minutes. **Esther** on the overlook enters you in the next heat of your
  bird's class or any class below: the first rider picks the course from three sprints (one lap, a minute or more)
  and three grands prix (three laps of two minutes or more, longer up the classes), and everyone
  else of that class or higher who sees her joins, taking an AI racer's stall. She announces the heat at
  two minutes and one minute and counts down the last ten seconds; be in the saddle. You are
  carried to the stalls, given a moment to settle, then a big five-second countdown starts the
  heat. Every AI bird has a kin jockey up; the crowd in the stands follows you round. Every course is a proper circuit on its own themed sky island: straights, sweepers,
  hairpins, chicanes and hills, striped kerbs, a grandstand full of fans in the infield. Six
  racers; from Class B on, Jolo and Teiyo are in the field.
* **Boost strips** on every course: run over the chevrons for a burst of speed.
* From Class B the courses carry features across the direct line with a slower road around:
  river birds walk the water, climbers go over the ridge, the Nether bird and Gold cross lava,
  everyone else goes round. Class A adds bogs that slow everyone; A and S stack more of it.
* **Rook the Bookie** takes one bet per heat. See him before Esther starts it.
* **Sable the Duel Master** runs two-rider races outside the ladder with an optional GP side
  bet. **Pell the Broker** swaps birds between players or hands over a gift.
* Stalls, all priced in **GP**: Sage Wynn (greens), Bilo the Nutkeeper (nuts), Tack (saddles,
  lures), the Fair (the Almanac, pocketwatches, fireworks, leads) and **Marl's GP Exchange**.

Classes run C → B → A → S. Three first-place finishes promote; a bird never drops a class.
Armored birds cannot race.

## Equipment

Sneak-click your bird with an empty hand (or press your inventory key while riding) for its
equipment: a saddle slot, an armor slot and saddlebags. **Chocobo Armor** comes in leather,
iron, diamond and netherite; iron and diamond birds wear it visibly. You can fight from the
saddle: your swings and arrows never hit your own bird.
**Saddlebags** add fifteen slots and take their name from the anvil.

Advancements track the whole line: first tame, first ride, first hatch, Green or
Blue, Black, Gold, finding a farm, reaching Whiskerwind, a first-place finish and
Class S.

The Nether and End birds wear lava and end stone in their crest, ruff and tail; they breed
true, and no nut turns them into a farm-line colour.

Java 21. `./gradlew test`, `./gradlew runVerification` (headless GameTests), then `./gradlew build`.
