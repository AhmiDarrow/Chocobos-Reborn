# Chocobos Reborn 1.1.0 - Full Grid

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21, client and server.

**Install this jar on the server and on every rider.**

**Forty-eight courses**

- **Twice the courses.** Every class now has twelve: six sprints and six
  grands prix. Class C gets farmland, Class B savanna, Class A giant
  mushrooms and Class S the deep dark, alongside the themes it already had.
  Every course has its own shape and its own landmark.
- **Sprints are long, grands prix are short.** A sprint is one long lap. A
  grand prix is three, four or five laps of a short, tight circuit, with the
  most laps on the shortest. A grand prix runs about a third longer than a
  sprint of its class, not twice as long.
- **Built like real circuits.** The short grands prix borrow from real racing:
  hairpins, chicanes, esses, crests and a climb into turn one, with the
  boost strips on corner exits.
- The course picker shows every course by class, with its laps, its length
  and what a win is worth.
- Existing worlds rebuild each course island the next time it is raced.
  Leftovers from the old, larger islands are cleared away.

**Racers are solid**

- **No more racing through each other.** Birds bump. Side by side, both are
  pushed apart and keep their pace. Run into a bird's tail and you lose pace
  for a moment while it barely feels it. A dashing or boosted bird shoves
  harder. A clean pass costs nothing.
- Birds on the grid, finished birds pulling aside and birds being set back
  onto the road pass through everyone.
- The AI picks gaps, passes on the side with room, eases off when it's boxed
  in, and the leader covers the inside without weaving.

**Points by distance**

- **A win is worth what the heat is long.** A sprint win is 4 points. A grand
  prix win scores by its length against the class's shortest sprint.
  A grand prix win is 5 or 6. 36 points promote: nine sprint wins, or six
  to eight grand prix wins.
- **The purse follows.** A sprint pays the class base and a grand prix the
  base scaled the same way. Second and third still take half and a quarter.
- After a ranked win you're told what it earned, like "+5 points (26 of 36)".
- Birds keep their share of the way up: points toward promotion are converted
  once to the new scale.

**Smarter, fairer AI**

- Jolo and Teiyo now pace off your bird's colour as well as its grade and
  training, so every colour has a fair shot at beating them.
- Field birds run at their class's pace, sprints no longer burn the bar off
  the grid, the AI lines up for boost strips, and stumbles happen at the
  intended rate.
- AI birds find their way round every shortcut on the short laps, climb every
  ridge they can take, and get themselves unstuck.

**Cleaner courses**

- No wall, rail, pool rim, post or decoration stands in a racing lane.
- During a race, climbing birds climb only the ridge shortcuts, not rails or
  walls, and every bird can step up a painted kerb on a hill.
- Detour exits have a flared rail, so a bird running wide slides back onto
  the road.
- Riders and the server agree about landings after a step, so a fast bird is
  never held in place by the server.

**Also**

- Flame and Purple birds don't race. Esther, Sable and every race start turn
  them away.
