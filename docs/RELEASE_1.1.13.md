# Chocobos Reborn 1.1.13 - Every Item Explains Itself

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21, client and server.

**Hover anything and it tells you what it is for**

- Gysahl Seeds, the Chocobo Saddle, GP, the gysahl crop and wild gysahl, the Square Gate and the Boost Pad now carry a grey hint line: where it comes from, what it does, how to use it. The greens, nuts, lure, whistle, almanac, pocketwatch, armor and saddlebags already had theirs.
- Hint lines wrap to the tooltip instead of running off the screen.
- The mods list shows the mod's CurseForge page and issue tracker beside its logo.
- A bird tamed by any means other than Gysahl Greens, a command or another mod, is on the whistle's list and in the almanac's ledger from that moment.
