Chocobos Reborn

Final Fantasy VII chocobos for Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, and Java 21. Catch a wild yellow with Gysahl Greens, train it on the eight greens, mate it with the eight nuts, walk the farm line to Green, Blue, Black and Gold, and race at Chocobo Square.

Original work. Original birds, original wark, original art. A fan work — not affiliated with Square Enix.

The birds

Adults stand about 1.8× a player, crest to the ground. Chicobos hatch small and grow through three stages. Eight plumages: Yellow, Green, Blue, White, Black, Gold, Purple and Flame. Females wear a shorter crest.

Wild yellows roam the Overworld. Wonderful yellows only show up on cold pads — snow and ice. Flame chocobos are Nether-born. Purple chocobos live on the End's outer islands. Hold a Chocobo Lure and wild birds come to you and glow, so you can spot tracks across a field.

Greens (training)

Gysahl, Krakka, Tantal, Pahsana, Curiel, Mimett, Reagan, Sylkis. Gysahl tames a wild bird (about one in three tries) and heals a hurt one. Every green trains speed, stamina, intelligence or cooperation; a bird eventually gets sated on each kind, so move up the ladder. Feeding also heals a little, restores stamina, and grows a chick faster. A sated bird gets one feed of each green back per day. Gysahl grows from seeds found in grass. Better greens come from Sage Wynn at the Square, race prizes, and lucky harvests.

Nuts (mating)

Pepio, Luchile, Saraha, Lasan, Pram, Porov, Carob, Zeio. Feed a nut to each of two owned adults of opposite sex and they mate. Plain nuts hatch a chick of the parents' colour whose talent follows the nut. Carob and Zeio change the line:

  1. Two Good-or-better Yellows + Carob → Green (mountain) or Blue (river).
  2. Green + Blue + Carob → Black. A missed roll hatches White.
  3. Black + Wonderful Yellow + Zeio → Gold. Gold never hatches without Zeio.

As in FF7, colour breeding needs racers: both parents must have first-place finishes at Chocobo Square before a Carob or Zeio can change the line (1 each for Green/Blue, 2 each for Black, 3 each for Gold). More wins make the roll surer until it is certain (4 combined firsts for Green/Blue, 9 for Black, 12 for Gold). A bird that has not raced enough tells you so when you hand it the nut. Purple breeds true by inheritance; no nut makes a Purple. Carob Nuts fall from ravagers; Zeio Nuts from piglin brutes.

Riding

Craft a Chocobo Saddle, put it on an owned adult, and right-click to ride. Sprint to dash on stamina; ease off to recover. Terrain follows FF7:

  • Green — climbs mountains (steps up two blocks)
  • Blue — walks on shallow rivers (≤ 3 blocks deep); the rider breathes underwater
  • White — climbs and crosses shallow water
  • Black — climbs, crosses shallow water, and sees at night
  • Gold — everything: mountains, any water (ocean), fire, night sight, and it flies (hold jump to flap, look up while moving to climb). Only Gold flies.
  • Purple — End bird: climbs, crosses any water, rider water breathing and slow falling. No flight.
  • Flame — shrugs off fire, walks on lava, and shares fire resistance with the rider

Sneak with an empty hand to take the saddle back.

Chocobo Farm

Chocobo Farms dot the plains, meadows and savannas: a barn, a paddock with a couple of wild yellows, a gysahl patch, a Stablehand in the barn selling greens and nuts, and a Farmhand at the gate who can send a saddled rider on to the Square.

Chocobo Square

Ride up to the Farmhand at any Chocobo Farm on a saddled bird, or place a Square Gate, and you are taken to the Square. There:

  • Esther starts a ranked heat for your bird's class. Tap for the first course, sneak for the second. Five racers line up against you; from Class B on, Jolo and Teiyo are in the field.
  • Rook the Bookie takes one bet per heat: empty hand cycles your pick, then hand him 1–16 GP.
  • The two course gates run a friendly one-on-one; the return gate or Esther (on foot) sends you home.
  • Stalls, all priced in GP won on the course: Sage Wynn (greens), Bilo the Nutkeeper (nuts), Tack (saddles, lures), the Fair (Sage's Notes, fireworks, leads), and Marl's GP Exchange, who swaps GP for everyday goods.
  • A sandstone plaza, stall booths, Rook's kiosk, and stands full of race fans who wave when a heat is on.

Classes run C → B → A → S. Three first-place finishes promote; a bird never drops a class. Sprint dashes drain stamina, so pace the bird. Eight courses: Meadow and Sprint (C), Ridge and Channel (B), Split and Tide (A), Void and Crown (S).

Start your journey

  1. Find Gysahl Seeds in grass and grow a patch.
  2. Find a wild yellow — or walk a plains farm — and tame it with Gysahl Greens.
  3. Craft a Chocobo Saddle (leather, string, iron) and ride.
  4. Craft a Chocobo Lure (amethyst around Gysahl) to draw the next wild bird.
  5. Ride a saddled bird to a Farmhand, or craft a Square Gate, and race. Colour breeding needs those firsts.
  6. Spend GP on better greens and nuts. Walk the farm line toward Gold.

Advancements track the whole line: first tame, first ride, first hatch, Green or Blue, Black, Gold, finding a farm, reaching the Square, a first-place finish, and Class S.

Requirements

  • Minecraft: 1.21.1
  • Loader: NeoForge 21.1.249
  • Java: 21
  • Side: Client + Server

No other mods required. Works standalone.

Standalone and Ninjacat Skies

Chocobos Reborn works standalone. In Ninjacat Skies it sits beside Tribal Power: Square keepers wear Tribal Kin looks, and the farm-line colours follow the tribes (Sprout, Stone, Claw, Spindle, Spark) with Purple on the End. The pack is optional.
