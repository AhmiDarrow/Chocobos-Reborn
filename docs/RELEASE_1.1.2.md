# Chocobos Reborn 1.1.2 - Chicobos

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21, client and server.

**Install this jar on the server and on every rider.**

**They are chicobos**

- A baby chocobo is a chicobo, everywhere: the almanac, the hatch
  advancement, tooltips, the nursery and its sign all said foal or chick.

**The farm line keeps its word**

- A Green and a Blue fed a Carob no longer hatch a White when neither has won
  in Whiskerwind. Without the wins the nut changes nothing, as the almanac
  always said: the chicobo takes a parent's colour. With the wins, a missed
  roll is still a White.

**Duels stake what they say**

- The duel picker only offers stakes the course can hold: it stops at the
  class's biggest purse, and each course shows the exact side bet it takes.
- Accepting a duel whose challenger has walked away now tells you so instead
  of doing nothing.

**The book and the signs**

- The almanac matches the game again: grands prix run a fifth to two fifths
  longer than a sprint, lava sits on some Class A and S courses, Rook's limit
  is the purse over the odds, duel stakes stop at the purse, ridden birds bump
  outside heats, Carob at Class S goes to first or second in a ranked heat,
  and Krakka and Tantal can be crafted.
- The welcome sends you home through the north gate, where it is. Nether and
  End birds go by those names everywhere, and the shortcut signs list only
  colours that can race. A Race Hall board line that ran off the sign fits.

**Fixes**

- Renaming a bird from the almanac is cleaned like an anvil rename, so a name
  can no longer carry formatting codes or control characters.
- A bird that failed to follow you across a dimension could wait far too long
  after you reopened a world in the same game session; it follows again.
- `/chocobosreborn race <n>` takes all twelve courses of a class.
