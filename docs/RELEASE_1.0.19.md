# Chocobos Reborn 1.0.19 - Back on track

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21, client and server.

**Install this jar on the server and on every rider.**

**Off the road**

- **A wide line costs nothing.** Leaving the road no longer throws away your
  lap. Swing wide, slide over a verge, come back on: the lap goes on.
- **A shortcut you shouldn't take sets you back, it doesn't finish you.** Cut
  further round than a wide line, stay off the road for four seconds, or go
  over the edge, and you're set down on the road where you left it and held
  for a second. You keep the lap. While you're off, the bar counts down.
- The AI racers follow the same rule.

**Shortcuts you can see**

- **Every shortcut is marked where the road forks.** A gantry in the
  feature's colour arches over the straight line, with banners in the colours
  of the breeds that can take it, a sign, and a coloured stripe down the road
  into it. Blue water, green ridges, orange lava. A brown gantry means a bog
  that slows everyone.
- **Your HUD calls it out.** On the way in you're told whether your bird can
  take it straight or should go round the outside, with a chime when it can.
- Each course picks up its markers the next time a race runs there.

**Old birds**

Birds from older versions join the current rules. The first time a bird
saved by an earlier version loads, it is converted once, and only once.

- **Race progress keeps its share.** First-place marks toward the old
  three-win promotion become points of nine. One mark is 3 points, two marks
  are 6. A Class S bird shows the full ladder.
- **Birds from before bloodlines get born stats.** A bird with no born stats
  at all rolls them from its birth grade, the same roll a wild bird gets
  today. A Wonderful bird starts ahead of a Poor one. Its greens training,
  wins, colour and grade stay as they were, so it races at least as well as
  before, and its foals now inherit a real bloodline.

A bird that won one or two sprints on 1.0.17 or 1.0.18 looks the same as an
old bird to this conversion, so it gets the same lift.
