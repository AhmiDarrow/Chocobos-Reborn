# Chocobos Reborn 1.0.18 - Race day

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21, client and server.

**Install this jar on the server and on every rider.** The crowd is drawn by
each rider's client, and the town is rebuilt by the server.

**The birds**

- **Other birds' legs move again.** 1.0.17 froze the stride of every bird your
  client does not drive, AI racers and other riders alike.

**The field**

- **Class C is a race now.** The C field runs as Good birds and cruises just
  under a fresh Good bird with no training. You have to use your dash to win.
- **Teiyo and Jolo run off your bird.** At the grid they take their pace from
  the best rider's own bird: Teiyo matches it in B, and runs 5 % over in A and
  10 % over in S. Jolo runs a little behind Teiyo. They never run slower than
  the field, and a gap during the heat still changes nothing. In 1.0.17 a
  Class B Teiyo was far faster than anyone could be at that level.
- **The right birds in the right class.** Jolo rides a Blue in B, a White in A
  and a Gold only in S. Class B regulars are Yellow, Green and Blue. No regular
  rides a Gold.

**The courses**

- **Stands on both sides of the track, by class.** Three stands on a Class C
  course, five on B, seven on A and ten on S, with about 40 fans on a C course
  and about 220 on S. Class S stands are grander: taller walls, a roof and flags.
- **The crowd costs the server nothing.** Fans are drawn by your own client,
  with no entities and no network traffic, and they get simpler with distance.
  Near fans cheer when the birds come past.
- Each course gets its new stands the next time a race runs there.

**Whiskerwind**

- **Half again as big.** Everything you know keeps its look and spreads out.
  New: a ranch where the town flock grazes, a nest barn with a chick nursery,
  a jockey lounge, a pond with a dock, an orchard, eight cottages, fruit and
  fish stalls, signposts, and a winners' board by the arch with the last ranked
  winner of every class.
- **Townsfolk.** Ten townsfolk keep their own day: home at night, work in the
  morning, the fountain in the afternoon, the inn in the evening. Click one for
  a word or a racing tip.
- **Race day.** The inn bell rings the heat calls and the start, the townsfolk
  line the overlook to cheer, and a rider's ranked win lights fireworks over
  the plaza.
- **The practice gates are gone.** Pick a course with Esther, or race a friend
  at Sable's.
- Look closely at the fountain.

Your first visit rebuilds the town. It takes a few seconds, and you wait above
the plaza while it does.
