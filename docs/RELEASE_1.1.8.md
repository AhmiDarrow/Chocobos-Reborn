# Chocobos Reborn 1.1.8 - Two Heats in Three

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21, client and server.

**Install this jar on the server and on every rider.**

**The named rivals race more often**

- Ahmi and Risika in Class C, and Teiyo and Jolo from Class B up, now turn up for about two ranked heats in three instead of one in two. When they sit out, field birds still take their two stalls.
- Nothing else about the roll changed: Esther rolls it when she posts the heat, the heat keeps it through a restart, and Rook hands back a stake on a rival who sits the heat out.
