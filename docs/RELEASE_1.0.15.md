# Chocobos Reborn 1.0.15 - Long bloodlines

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21, client and server. A dash that
runs dry has to recover, and a perfect bird is a long line.

**Install this jar on the server and on every rider.** The dash key, the
lock, and the born stats are read on both sides. A guest still on 1.0.14
keeps treating forward as sprint, so their dash will not follow the key.

**Stamina**

- **An empty bar has to climb.** Once a dash spends the last point, dash
  stays off until stamina is back to 50. Holding the key does not nibble the
  recovering bar. While it climbs you ride at a normal pace. The slow crawl
  is only while the bar is empty. The race line says when the dash is waiting
  on 50. Field racers follow the same lock.
- **The dash follows the sprint key.** Holding forward alone no longer keeps
  the bar empty. Let go of sprint and it climbs, even if you keep riding.

**Intelligence**

- **A smart bird spends stamina more slowly.** On every other tick it may
  keep the point. At 100 intelligence half of the drain is free. At 0
  nothing is skipped.
- **Boost pads listen to the same stat.** With no intelligence they are
  still +55% for 50 ticks. At 100 intelligence they are +75% for 70 ticks.
  Field racers use the same pads.

**Breeding**

- **The almanac shows what is born and what you fed.** Bright is the
  bloodline. Pale is greens. Greens still make this bird fast. About a tenth
  of what you feed sticks in the next foal.
- **The line climbs over many clutches.** Feed a blank bird full and it
  offers 10. Breed that blood down the middle and feed each chick full:
  eight generations still offer 59, and twenty reach 85. Most chicks land a
  little under the blood, so you breed the pair again.
- **A legendary stat is rare.** One chick in eight sparks one stat toward
  the stronger parent and gains 5. A born stat above 96 takes that spark,
  and only when both parents already offer 85 or more in that stat. A nut
  or a lucky roll on a lower line stops at 96.
- **Nuts are a small gift.** The nut's tier is added to speed and stamina,
  and half of that, rounded down, to mind and cooperation. Pepio is +1.
  Zeio is +8 and +4. A Wonderful grade floor is 8.
- **Birds you already own keep their training.** Born stats they never had
  read as none, so they still race on their greens. The next foal inherits
  about a tenth of those greens. Colour breeding, wins, and grades are
  unchanged.

Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.249 / Java 21.
