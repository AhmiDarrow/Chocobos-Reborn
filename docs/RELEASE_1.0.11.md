# Chocobos Reborn 1.0.11 - Fine feathers

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21, client and server. Your birds shed
feathers, and a full bird still eats a green when it is hurt.

**Feathers**

- **Tame birds shed.** Each of your adult chocobos drops a feather every 5 to 10
  minutes. Chicks, wild birds, the Square's town birds and birds in a heat do not.
- **Brush your bird.** Use a brush on your own adult chocobo and a feather comes loose
  on the spot, with hearts. Each stroke costs 16 durability, like brushing an armadillo.

**Greens**

- **Greens heal a full bird.** A bird that is sated on a green, or still digesting the
  last one, used to refuse it outright. If it is hurt it now eats the green and heals,
  without any training. At full health it still refuses, so no green is wasted.
