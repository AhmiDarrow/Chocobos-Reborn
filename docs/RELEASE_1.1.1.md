# Chocobos Reborn 1.1.1 - Fair Odds

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21, client and server.

**Install this jar on the server and on every rider.**

**Riders bump everywhere**

- Two ridden birds pass through each other no more. A ridden bird is a
  vehicle, and the game never pushes a vehicle, so outside a heat riders could
  ride straight through one another. The kart contact that racers already use
  in a heat now runs for any bird a player is riding, so riders bump in the
  paddock, on the square and on the road as well as on the course.

**Rook never pays more than the purse**

- A bet can no longer earn more than winning the heat does. The most Rook takes
  on a pick is the heat's first-place purse divided by the odds, so the biggest
  payout equals the purse: 3 GP on yourself in a Class C sprint, 1 GP on the
  field at six to one, up to the sixteen a hand can hold on a long Class S
  grand prix. Rook tells you the limit with the odds, and if a bet placed
  before the heat was known is over its limit, the difference comes back.
- A duel pot is capped the same way: the stake stops at the course's purse, so
  the winner nets one purse and the loser is out one.
- A lost bet pays nothing, and a heat that ran to a result settles every bet.
  Before, a heat no rider finished handed every spectator their stake back even
  when a field bird had won; now only a heat nobody at all finished scratches.
