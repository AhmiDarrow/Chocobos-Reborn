# Chocobos Reborn 1.0.9 - Follow me

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21, client and server. A tame bird's first
order is Follow, and Follow means it comes with you.

**Follow**

- **A new tame follows.** Feeding a wild bird Gysahl Greens used to sit it down. It
  stands and follows you instead. Stay and Wander are still a right-click away, and a
  bird you had already told to wander still wanders when it stands up from Stay.
- **Follow crosses worlds.** A bird on Follow comes with you into the Nether, the End,
  Whiskerwind, and back, and lands on open ground a couple of blocks behind you. Stay,
  Wander, a lead, a race, and the Square's own birds stay where you left them. A bird
  whose chunk is not loaded makes the trip once that chunk loads. If you were riding
  when you crossed, mount again on the other side.

Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.249 / Java 21.
