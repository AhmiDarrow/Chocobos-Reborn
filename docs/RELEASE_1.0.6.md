# Chocobos Reborn 1.0.6 - Fair bets

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249, Java 21, client and server. A bug-fix release; existing saves, farms and courses are untouched.

- **No more stranding on the way to Whiskerwind.** Going to or from the Square while in a boat, on a horse or in a minecart used to leave you stuck to a vehicle in the other world, often over the void. You now get off first.
- **Teleports work while riding a flier.** The lock that keeps you on an airborne bird now only stops you sneaking off. It no longer blocks /tp, waystones and other teleports.
- **Fair betting.** A racer can only bet on themselves in a heat they are running. The Field pays by how many birds it covers, down to 1 to 1 on a Class C card. Before, you could bet the Field, finish last and collect six times your stake.
- **GP survives a crash.** Bookie bets, duel stakes and open duel challenges are saved. After a crash they are paid back the next time you log in or enter the Square. Race courses a crash left loaded are released when the server starts.
- **A traded bird's gear stays with the bird.** The equipment screen closes for anyone who no longer owns the bird, so the old owner can't strip its saddle, armour or bags after a trade, gift or release.
- **Smaller fixes.**
  - Dying mid-race no longer puts you back on your bird.
  - A duel pot goes to the rider who stayed, not back to the one who quit.
  - Saddlebag slots take nothing when the bird wears no bags.
